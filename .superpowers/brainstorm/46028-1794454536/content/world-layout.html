How should the projects and the office fit together?

Two ways to lay out the main screen. Rough sketches only: look at the arrangement, not the colours. Click the one that feels closer.

A. One city

Every project is a building in one small city. The office is one of the buildings. Click a project to see its people; click the office to go inside and see the floors.

B. Side by side

Projects on the left, the office floor on the right, both always visible. Pick a project and its team's zone lights up, with the people who commit to it listed underneath.

What differs

A. One city

  • Stronger first impression on stage
  • Feels like exploring the company

Cost

  • Camera moves and a scene change to go inside the office: the riskiest thing to build in under 24 hours
  • The project-to-seat link takes two steps to see

B. Side by side

  • The whole point is visible in one click: project, people, where they sit
  • Three people can build the three panels in parallel

Cost

  • Less of a wow moment; closer to a dashboard
  • Gets cramped past roughly 20 projects
